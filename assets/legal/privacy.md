# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 31 October 2026

This policy explains what information séance (“the app”, “we”, “us”) collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It covers the séance mobile apps and the séance website.

séance is run by **[OPERATOR NAME]**. You can reach us at **[CONTACT EMAIL]**.

## The short version

- You sign in with your **phone number** only. We don’t ask for your name, email, location, contacts or payment details.
- We store your **practice progress, bookmarks and journal entries** so they stay in sync across your devices.
- We **don’t sell** your data, show ads, or use your data for advertising.
- You can **delete your account and all of its data** at any time from **Profile → Delete account**.

## What we collect

**Your phone number.** We use it to create your account and to sign you in with a one-time code sent by SMS. Your number is your login; it is not shown to other people.

**Account identifiers.** Our sign-in provider gives your account a random ID, which we use to keep your data separate from everyone else’s.

**What you do in the app.** To show your progress and keep it in sync, we store:

- which practices you marked as done, and on which dates;
- the date you started the programme (used to unlock each week);
- the readings and hymns you bookmark;
- the journal reflections you write, and the practice each one is attached to.

Journal entries can be personal, and some may reveal your religious beliefs or how you are feeling. They are private to your account. We don’t read them, analyse them, or use them for anything except showing them back to you.

**Information stored only on your device.** Your Bible translation, the chapter you were last reading and similar preferences stay on your device and are not sent to us.

**Technical information.** When you sign in, our sign-in provider processes technical information such as your IP address, device and browser type, and a security check (reCAPTCHA on the web, or the platform’s app-integrity check on phones). This is used to deliver the code and to prevent abuse.

**What we don’t collect.** We don’t collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, payment details, or advertising identifiers.

## How we use information

- To create your account and sign you in.
- To store your progress, bookmarks and journal, and sync them between your devices.
- To work out which programme week you have reached.
- To keep the service secure and prevent misuse (for example, too many sign-in attempts).
- To answer you when you contact us.

**Reminders.** The weekly reading reminder is scheduled on your own device. We don’t send it from our servers and it doesn’t require any information about you. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

We rely on your consent (when you create an account and use the app) and on our legitimate interest in running a secure, working service. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

## Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that run séance, and only for the purposes above:

- **Google Firebase** (Google LLC) provides sign-in (including sending SMS codes through mobile carriers), our database (Cloud Firestore), web hosting and server functions. See the [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **YouTube** (Google LLC). Videos in the Watch section are played with YouTube’s embedded player in privacy-enhanced mode. When you play a video, YouTube may collect information under its own terms. See [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Wikipedia / Wikimedia Foundation.** Readings in the Library are loaded directly from Wikipedia by your device, so Wikimedia receives your IP address and browser information. See the [Wikimedia Privacy Policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy).

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

We never sell your personal information.

## Where your information is stored

Your account and data are stored by Google Firebase on servers that may be located outside Ghana, including in the United States. Google protects this data with the safeguards described in its policies.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your progress, bookmarks and journal are erased from our database and your sign-in account is removed straight away. Our providers may keep limited records (such as security logs) for a short period as described in their own policies.

## Your rights and choices

Under Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and similar laws, you can:

- **see** the data we hold about you (all of it is visible in the app);
- **correct** it (you can edit or delete journal entries and bookmarks yourself);
- **delete** it, together with your account, from **Profile → Delete account**;
- **ask for a copy** of your data, or ask us anything about it, by writing to **[CONTACT EMAIL]**;
- **complain** to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana, or to the data protection authority where you live.

## Security

Data is encrypted while travelling between your device and our servers (HTTPS/TLS) and is stored on Google’s infrastructure. Our database rules allow each account to read and write only its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone and SIM protected, because whoever can receive SMS on your number can sign in to your account.

## Children

séance is not intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
